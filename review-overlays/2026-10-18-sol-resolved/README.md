# Separate final Ukrainian translation overlays

The corpus overlay reviews all 125,264 original records / 199,737 segments. It preserves the original dictionary draft and uses the latest accepted authored decisions. Held whole records remain excluded from qualified translations. Actual Astra and Sol attribution is retained; newly resolved or changed final translations received a different Sol validator. Human validation and linguistic-accuracy certification are not claimed.

The 1,758 English app-baseline meanings are a separate source universe. Their Chinese headword, reading and English definition fields come from the CC-CEDICT-derived English headword table; the actual upstream snapshot metadata and CC BY-SA 4.0 attribution are included. Their original English input, actual database hash and row identity are preserved. Their translations do not increase the 125,264-record original-corpus coverage count. Qualified and held baseline records are separate files.

Runtime availability is narrower than original-source coverage. The coverage file accounts for all 124,725 actual English/French app-baseline rows, genuine unresolved entries, 2,297 original records without a strict app counterpart, and aggregate coverage counts for all 13 bundled HSK lists. Any/all matched reading coverage does not claim a preferred reading; counts of terms absent from the existing English/French baseline are separate. Word-list membership, ordering and per-term app IDs are omitted because their separate redistribution lineage remains unresolved. The complete term/readings replay stays in the private independent audit referenced by hash. The public validator checks the aggregate schema and arithmetic; it cannot replay the omitted private membership or certify official HSK completeness.

The source corpus and derived Ukrainian adaptations retain CC BY-SA 4.0 attribution to CC-CEDICT contributors, MDBG, the original CEDICT project by Paul Andrew Denisowski, and the Ukrainian CEDICT project. Separate app-baseline-derived adaptations retain their actual baseline source provenance. No Tatoeba example texts or audio are included in this dictionary overlay package.

Run validate.py with this directory and --source pointing to the unchanged original dictionary.json. It replays source/text bindings, whole-record eligibility, separate baseline identity and Sol validation lineage. Original private review hashes remain references; private task envelopes, logs and local paths are omitted. This public projection cannot independently certify model authorship, linguistic accuracy, App Store availability or runtime approval. App packaging and Store delivery have separate receipts.
